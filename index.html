+ReMind

Check your cognitive health in 5 minutes.

ReMind is a free early screening for Alzheimer's disease. Answer a short questionnaire. Our model, trained on real patient data from the National Alzheimer's Coordinating Center, estimates your risk level from your age, mood, and behavior.

If your risk is high, you get brain games and tracking to share with your doctor. If it is low, you get regular check-ins so changes are caught early.

ReMind is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. It does not replace a doctor. If you are worried about memory changes, talk to a healthcare professional.
